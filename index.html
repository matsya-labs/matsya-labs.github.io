A small personal lab for software made from India, for Indian families and civic life. Built with care for language, ownership and the long run.

The name

Matsya, the fish, is the first of Vishnu's avatars. Manu found it tiny in his cupped hands, and it outgrew every vessel he gave it, from a pot to a lake to the sea. When the great flood came, it carried Manu's boat to safety on the horn on its head, and with it the seeds of life and the knowledge of the Vedas. That is the ambition here: start small, keep growing, and carry what matters.

Work

A few projects are in development, all private for now: some for families and the stories they keep, some for public conversation. They will show up here once there is something to try.
